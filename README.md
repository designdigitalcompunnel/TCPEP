# TCPEP website (built on the HIF template)

Truro & Colchester Partnership for Economic Prosperity (TCPEP). Open `index.html` in a browser, keeping the `assets/` folder next to it.

- `index.html`: homepage (growing opportunity, about TCPEP, focus areas, discover the region, partners, physician resources promotion, contact, newsletter).
- `physician-resources.html`: Physician Resources overview (welcome, why choose Truro & Colchester, practice / live / settling-in categories, next steps, communities, settling-in timeline, information, community stories, and a contact form).
- `assets/`: TCPEP logo (`tcpep-logo.jpg`) and favicons generated from the arrow mark.

Template design, layout, section order, animations, accessibility panel and read-aloud are unchanged. Brand colours follow the TCPEP palette: primary blue #0750A5, dark blue #073A78, growth green #58A65C, progress cyan #35B5CF, headings #1F2937, body #4B5563, light background #F7F9FC.

## Navigation
"Physician Resources" opens the physician page. Its dropdown lists Practice Here, Live Here and Settling In as planned destinations (not links). No extra pages were created for them.
