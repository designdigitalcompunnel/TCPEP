Welcome

Welcome to Truro & Colchester

TCPEP helps prospective physicians, medical residents and families explore practice opportunities and life in Truro and Colchester County. We offer local information, community connections and guidance to appropriate recruitment resources.

  • Practice Here

    Local medical practice settings, including Colchester East Hants Health Centre, practice opportunities, licensing information, and recruitment resources.

  • Live Here

    Housing, schools, childcare, recreation, culture, transportation, and community life.

  • Settling In

    Relocation guidance, local services, orientation, family connections, and community support.

  • What TCPEP does

    TCPEP provides community support and information. It does not make medical licensing, immigration, or formal recruitment decisions.

Contact TCPEP for current information about practice, housing, and relocation. Contact TCPEP

    How TCPEP can help you

    The steps below describe general areas of support. They are not a fixed process. TCPEP provides community support and information; it does not make medical licensing, immigration, or formal recruitment decisions.

    1. 01

      Get in touch

      Tell us about your practice goals and family needs.

    2. 02

      Explore your options

      Learn about practice settings, housing, schools, and community life.

    3. 03

      Plan your move

      Review relocation guidance and local services.

    4. 04

      Make local connections

      Find out about local networks that can help you and your family.

    5. 05

      Settle in

      Explore orientation and community support as you arrive.

    6. 06

      Stay connected
