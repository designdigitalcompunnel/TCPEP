07 — Purpose

Our purpose

TCPEP promotes regional economic prosperity, business growth, workforce attraction and retention, and community development across Truro and Colchester County.

Economic prosperityPromoting growth that benefits people and businesses across the region.
Business growthSupporting local businesses to start, expand, and hire.
Workforce attractionHelping people discover careers here, and stay.
Community developmentBuilding vibrant, connected communities for everyone.
Our purpose is to bring partners together to grow opportunity and strengthen communities across our region.

Our focusRegional economic development in partnership with municipal, provincial, business and community partners.
